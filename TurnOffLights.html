Each room starts with a few random dark spots; the rest glow softly. Tapping a tile toggles it and its N, E, S, W neighbours — lit ones go dark with a pop, dark ones light back up. Every room is guaranteed solvable. Par is the fewest moves possible.
Every room has an ID — share it (or the link) and friends get the exact same board. The Daily room is the same for everyone worldwide each day (UTC).
